                                       Email: yichenl [at] mit [dot] edu

I am a fourth-year Ph.D. student at MIT CSAIL working with Prof. Antonio Torralba and Prof. Wojciech Matusik.

My research lies in computer vision and deep learning. I am interested in capturing states of the world through measurement that uncovers the underlying nature through representation. My research spans the spectrum from multisenosry perception to sensing hardware.

Before coming to MIT, I have been fortunate to worked with Prof. Leonidas Guibas and Prof. Gordon Wetzstein at Stanford. I also worked with Prof.Emily Whiting and Prof. Kate Saenko.
